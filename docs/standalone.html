Left click reveals · right click (or flag mode) flags.
Cells are tinted from grey to red by the estimated chance they hide a mine.
Every percentage is exact — the fraction of consistent mine layouts with a mine there. If the search cannot finish, the cells read ? rather than a guess.
Show picks which estimate you see: the solver's proof, the network's guess (purple), or both — the guess in the top-left of each cell, the proof in the bottom-right.
Auto-play deductions opens every cell proven safe and flags every cell proven to be a mine. On neural network only it follows the network instead, opening under 5% and flagging over 95% — which is how you find out what the network is worth, and it will eventually open a mine.
